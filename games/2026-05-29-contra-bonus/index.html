⚔ CONTRA

Run & Gun

🕹 MOVE & AIM
Use the D-Pad (bottom-left) to move left/right. Press ↑ to aim up, ↗ for diagonal!
🦘 JUMP
Tap the green A button (bottom-right) to jump. You can shoot while jumping!
🔫 SHOOT
Auto-fire is always on! Hold B button for extra fire speed. Tap right side of screen to aim up.
💀 DANGER
One hit = death! Collect red capsules for the Spread Gun. Survive to the BOSS!
