Tools & Techniques:

  • Core Engine: Three.js for rendering a deep space environment with metallic voxel grids.
  • Optimization: InstancedMesh is used to render a dense starfield and thousands of background asteroids without lagging.
  • Physics Engine: Cannon.js handles enemy routing physics and projectile intersection testing.
  • Architectural Genre: Tower Defense loop. Enemies follow a set path, towers auto-target and fire.
