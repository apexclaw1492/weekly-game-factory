VOXEL SANDBOX

Blocks Placed: 0 | Mode: PLACE

Rotate: Left Click + Drag | Pan: Right Click + Drag | Zoom: Scroll

Technical Stack: Three.js + Raycasting + Web Audio Synthesizer.

  • Voxel Raycasting: Intersects the grid and placed cubes using the normalized grid normals to append/remove geometric meshes dynamically.
  • Dynamic Materials: Incorporates a mix of Standard, Emissive, and Translucent materials with shadow mapping enabled for high fidelity.
  • Local Persistence: Automatically serializes the layout coordinates and colors of the blocks in localStorage so worlds persist across reloads.
  • Interactive Audio Synth: Generates satisfying sound sweeps on placements and block breaking by dynamically generating frequency envelopes.
