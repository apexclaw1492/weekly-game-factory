Learning Section

Tools & Techniques:

  • Core Engine: Three.js for rendering soft shadows and minimalist pastel materials.
  • Optimization: InstancedMesh is used to render a large volume of abstract floating background geometric shapes.
  • Physics Engine: Cannon.js handles a dynamic player sphere (rolling physics), jumping impulses, and physically interactive platforms.
  • Improvements: Added soft bloom post-processing, particle trails, double-jump prevention, and enhanced pastel lighting.
