Learning Section

Tools & Techniques:

  • Core Engine: Three.js for rendering a synthwave grid and glowing neon materials (MeshStandardMaterial with high emissive).
  • Optimization: InstancedMesh is used to render hundreds of neon skyscrapers efficiently in a single draw call.
  • Physics Engine: Cannon.js handles authentic gravity, collision detection, and rigid body dynamics for the player and obstacles.
  • Architectural Genre: An Endless Runner loop that continuously shifts objects backwards and recycles them to save memory.

LLM Used: Gemini 2.5 Pro
