⚔ CONTRA

Run & Gun

🕹 MOVE
Use the D-Pad (bottom-left) to run. Press ▲ to aim up, ▲+◀▶ for diagonal!
🦘 JUMP
Tap the A button (right side) to leap. Shoot while jumping!
🔫 SHOOT
Auto-fire is on by default. Press B for extra fire rate. Grab red capsules for Spread Gun!
💀 DANGER
One hit = death! 3 lives. Memorize enemy patterns. Survive to the BOSS!
